We're headed back to SFIFF! [_Red Razor on Soft Skin_](/project/red-razor-on-soft-skin.html) is an **Official Selection** of the [2026 Santa Fe International Film Festival](https://sfiff.eventive.org/films/6aaadeead5c4f683fead2ad0), as part of **New Mexico Shorts After Dark**.

Written, directed and edited by [**Luca Silver**](https://phantasmagoria.xyz), the film stars **Jeffrey M. Williams, Shelley Holt and John Dewar**. We shot this one on 16mm at McCall's Barbershop right here in Albuquerque -- a mother sends her timid son to a mysterious barber for a shave and a haircut. Spoiler: it goes badly.

Big congrats to Luca and the whole cast and crew, and thanks to the SFIFF programmers for making room for our little nightmare!
