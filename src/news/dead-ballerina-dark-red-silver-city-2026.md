Two pieces of festival news for [_Dead Ballerina_](/project/dead-ballerina.html)! Our animated music video for BLARF is an **Official Selection** of the [2026 Dark Red Film Festival](https://www.darkredhorror.com/), and it was awarded **Best Animation** at the [2026 Silver City Community Film Festival](https://silvercityfilmfest.org/).

The Best Animation award joins the film's [previously announced Audience Choice Award](/dead-ballerina-wins-the-audience-choice-award-at-the-2026-silver-city-community-film-festival.html) at Silver City.
