_Big Sword_ is headed to Austin! ⚔️

Our Albuquerque-grown psychedelic fantasy-horror project is one of the **2026 Fantastic Pitches Lab finalists**! Writer/director [Adrian Pijoan](https://adrianpijoan.net) and producer [Alonso Indacochea](https://www.instagram.com/alonso_inda/) will pitch the project live at [Fantastic Fest](https://fantasticfest.com/) on September 20.

[Fantastic Pitches](https://www.fantasticpitches.com), presented by Fantastic Fest and [Chroma](https://www.chromapresents.com/), is a live competition for micro-budget feature projects. This year, it's all about monster movies and creature features. The finalists work with mentors and meet with people in the industry before pitching to a jury and festival audience.

One project will be offered **$100,000 in production financing**, along with a distribution deal and a premiere at Fantastic Fest in 2027. For _Big Sword_, that award would be the money that gets the film made. Pretty huge!

<br>

### So, what is _Big Sword_?

Set in 2008, _Big Sword_ follows **Sadie**, a restless twenty-something who steals an enormous sword after a drug-fueled night. When the blade starts cutting open doors to other dimensions, Sadie and her best friend Jenna get pulled into a funny, bloody fight against the horrors that want it back.

> _"**Big Sword** started with the image of a messy young woman out partying with a giant sword. It's inspired by my own twenties, and the way we mythologize our chaotic coming-of-age experiences. Underneath the blood, monsters and dive bars, it's a story about anyone who has reached adulthood still waiting for the portal to open and the adventure to start."_
>
> — **Adrian Pijoan**

The monster on their heels is **the First Wound**: a bandaged, mutilated figure with a strapped-and-chained wound full of teeth. The team plans to make this world with practical creature effects, handmade animation, collage, rotoscoping and stop motion.

### The plan: make it by hand in Albuquerque

_Head of a Cow Productions_ is developing _Big Sword_, with Dust Wave lined up to support the future production. The team wants to keep it tactile and local, using practical creatures, handmade props, lived-in Albuquerque locations and animation to make the story's psychedelic world even stranger.

Dust Wave member [Rhiannon Barela](https://www.instagram.com/rbarelafilm/) designed the original **Big Sword** included in our Fantastic Pitches application. And yes, it really is that big.

Fellow Dust Wave member [Dusty Deen](https://www.dustydeen.com/) is attached as **animation director** and will mix handmade and digital techniques for planned supernatural sequences. [Zoë Ligon](https://www.instagram.com/thongria/) is attached as a **performer**.

When the project moves into production, Dust Wave plans to pitch in with space, equipment, crew and vendor coordination, locations, background casting and art-department resources. It's familiar territory for us; we've taken the same hands-on approach to projects including [_Movie Monsters_](/project/movie-monsters.html), [_Pandora's Greenhouse_](/project/pandora-s-greenhouse.html) and [_Red Razor on Soft Skin_](/project/red-razor-on-soft-skin.html).

> _"**Big Sword** is exactly the kind of ambitious, singular film I want to make in New Mexico, and getting to build it with Adrian and our collaborators at Dust Wave makes this opportunity especially exciting. At a moment of real change for our local industry, Fantastic Pitches gives us the chance to move this project closer to production and show what New Mexico filmmakers can create together."_
>
> — **Alonso Indacochea**

### Next stop: Fantastic Fest

Between now and the live event, the _Big Sword_ team will keep working on the screenplay and pitch with a Fantastic Pitches mentor. At the festival, the finalists will also meet with industry professionals before one project is chosen for financing.

_Big Sword_ is a **finalist -- not the award winner (at least not yet)**. But Austin gets the team one step closer to making this strange, handmade New Mexico monster movie for real. Huge congratulations to Adrian, Alonso, Dusty and everyone helping make it happen! 🎉⚔️
