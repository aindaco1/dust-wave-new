Our animated music video, [_Dead Ballerina_](/project/dead-ballerina.html), won the **Audience Choice Award** at the [2026 Silver City Community Film Festival](https://silvercityfilmfest.org/)! The film was also named an **Official Selection**.

_Dead Ballerina_ was directed and animated by [Dusty Deen](https://www.dustydeen.com/), written by [Alonso Indacochea](https://www.instagram.com/alonso_inda), with 3D animation by [Jay Renteria](https://www.instagram.com/jayrenteriuh).

Thank you to everyone who watched and voted, and to the festival organizers for selecting the film. Congratulations to Dusty, Alonso, and Jay! 🎉
