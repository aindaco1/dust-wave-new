Welcome to this week's *DIY Filmmaker Digest* — part syllabus, part mixtape, part fever dream of cinema's shifting ground. Below: essays and interviews that ask what artists are tinkering with, what systems are breaking, and how you might improvise your own way forward. There's a **major** New Mexico bias, plus podcasts, videos, and trailers to keep your queue weird. **DIY forever!**

But first: somehow, Dust Wave has entered a **very specific awards-season era.**

    <p>We've been sitting on this one and can finally say it: our Albuquerque-grown psychedelic fantasy-horror project <a href="/big-sword-named-a-2026-fantastic-pitches-lab-finalist.html"><em>Big Sword</em></a> is a <strong>2026 Fantastic Pitches Lab finalist</strong>! Writer/director <a href="https://adrianpijoan.net" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Adrian Pijoan</a> and yours truly will pitch it live at <a href="https://fantasticfest.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fantastic Fest</a> on September 20. The project that wins gets $100,000 in production financing, a Chroma distribution deal, and a 2027 Fantastic Fest premiere. So there is now a real, if slightly bonkers, path from "burnout steals a giant sword" to "we actually get to make this bizarre thing."</p>
    <p>It has been my great pleasure to befriend and work with Adrian over the past few years -- it's crazy that we're here with a chance to make our creative dreams come true. I'll say this: <em>I'm going to Austin with every intention of winning</em> and bringing back the financial resources that will further unlock what our community is capable of. Not just for me and Adrian, but <strong>for all of us in New Mexico making miracles in the high desert.</strong></p>
