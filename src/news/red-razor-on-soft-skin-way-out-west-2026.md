We're back at Way OUT West! [_Red Razor on Soft Skin_](/project/red-razor-on-soft-skin.html) is an **Official Selection** of the [2026 Way OUT West Film Fest](https://wayoutwestfilmfest.com/project/red-razor-on-soft-skin/), as part of the **Queers for Fears** shorts program.

Written, directed and edited by [**Luca Silver**](https://phantasmagoria.xyz), the film stars **Jeffrey M. Williams, Shelley Holt and John Dewar**. We shot this giallo short on 16mm at McCall's Barbershop right here in Albuquerque. A mother sends her timid son in for a shave and a haircut, and the barber has other plans.

Big congrats to Luca and the whole cast and crew! Thanks to the Way OUT West team for having us back.
