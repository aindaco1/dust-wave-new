_Dead Ballerina_ is an animated music video created for BLARF's [_Film Scores for Films That Don't Exist_ music video contest](https://www.stonesthrow.com/news/blarf-film-scores-for-films-that-dont-exist-music-video-contest/), presented by Stones Throw Records.

<br>

### Crew

Directed & Animated by Dusty Deen<br>
Written by Alonso Indacochea<br>
3D Animation by Jay Renteria

<br>

### Awards

[2026 Silver City Community Film Festival](https://silvercityfilmfest.org/)
* **Audience Choice Award**
* Official Selection
