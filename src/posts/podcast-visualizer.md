[_Podcast Visualizer_](https://github.com/aindaco1/podcast-visualizer) helps podcasters turn conversations into polished videos with animated captions, speaker-aware layouts, and a consistent visual style.
<br><br>

### Supported platforms

Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later.
<br><br>

### What creatives can do

* **Turn recordings into editable transcripts.** Bring in a local podcast recording, identify speakers, correct words, and approve the conversation before making a video.
* **Create animated caption videos.** Render full episodes or selected moments with readable, speaker-aware captions and a consistent visual rhythm.
* **Publish for different screens.** Make horizontal, square, and vertical versions for video platforms, feeds, and social posts.
* **Keep the podcast's identity.** Customize speaker names and project branding so a series can carry the same look from episode to episode.
* **Move into an editing workflow.** Export finished videos with a solid background or transparency for further compositing and editorial work.
* **Prepare episode chapters.** Review and export chapter markers for YouTube, show notes, or another publishing workflow.
<br><br>
