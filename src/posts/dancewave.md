_Dancewave: A Blast From the Past_ was Dust Wave's adults-only prom party at Juno Brewery. Every era collided in a night of retro hits, disco lights, vintage looks, and favorite prom anthems.

Guests came dressed for the prom they remembered—or the one they wished they had—and danced the night away with friends old and new.

<br>

### Event Details

**Saturday, September 6, 2025 at 7:00 PM**<br>
**Juno Brewery**<br>
