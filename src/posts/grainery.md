[_Grainery_](https://grainery.xyz) is a quiet screenwriting app that keeps formatting decisions from interrupting the draft. The interface stays focused on the story, and files stay with the writer.
<br><br>

### Supported platforms

macOS on Apple Silicon and Intel, Windows, and Linux.
<br><br>

### What creatives can do

* **Stay inside the draft.** Write action, scene headings, characters, parentheticals, dialogue, and transitions without stopping to format every line by hand.
* **Move quickly with screenplay-aware shortcuts.** Cycle between elements, let the editor recognize common headings and transitions, and always see what kind of line you are writing.
* **Keep scripts portable.** Save Grainery documents and export PDF, Fountain, or Final Draft files for collaborators, table reads, submissions, and production.
* **Shape the workspace to the project.** Plugins can add scene maps and numbering, character completion and renaming, review notes, word counts, custom styling, and other writer-focused tools.
* **Write without a subscription lock-in.** Files remain with the writer and can move into other screenplay workflows when needed.
<br><br>
