[_CutNotes_](https://github.com/aindaco1/cutnotes) turns spoken feedback on a rough cut into a transcript and organized notes for your editor. Watch the cut, say the timecodes aloud, and record your comments as you go. The notes bring together a general feedback summary and specific changes in timeline order.
<br><br>

### Supported platforms

Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later. Local note formatting with Apple Intelligence requires macOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and an available system language model. Transcription can run locally without Apple Intelligence.
<br><br>

### What creatives can do

* **Talk through a rough cut.** Choose a microphone and record feedback while you watch. Pause when you need to, then resume the same session.
* **Bring in an existing review.** Import an audio or video recording, or start with a plain-text transcript you already have.
* **Give your editor clear notes.** Turn spoken timecodes into consistent timestamps and organize comments into a summary followed by chronological notes.
* **Keep transcription on your Mac.** Set up Parakeet v3 once, then transcribe locally. English is fully supported; the other 24 available languages are experimental.
* **Choose how to finish.** Save a transcript on its own, format notes locally with Apple Intelligence, or explicitly choose an installed MacWhisper or Codex tool for its supported stage.
* **Keep your working material.** Each session gets its own files, and completed audio or transcripts remain available if a later step fails. Imported media stays untouched.
* **Work from the app or the terminal.** The native Mac app and command-line tool offer the same recording, import, and formatting workflows. Notes save as Markdown for sharing or further editing.
