To show someone how an edit works, open the little VHS camera in your Mac's menu bar, pick the edit window and talk them through it. Record saves the screen as a movie, your voice on one 24-bit WAV and the sound from the Mac on another. If the scene drowns out your explanation, you can turn it down later.

You can also record a whole display or just a corner of it, take a screenshot straight to the clipboard, or leave the screen out and record sound. For an audio-only interview with the Zoom app open, Record takes the raw mic signal by default so its voice processing stays out of Zoom's way.

### When all you need is the text

Choose **Quick Dictation**, say the thing you meant to write down, then read it back and copy it. Longer recordings live in **Sessions**, where you can find an interview by title or date, play it back and copy the transcript. If you already have a folder of interviews, import the audio files together. The originals stay where they are.

Transcription runs on your Mac after a one-time Parakeet download. Teach Record the spelling of a name it keeps getting wrong and you can apply that spelling to an old interview without transcribing it again. The raw transcript stays available. You can also start or stop a recording with a keyboard shortcut, Apple Shortcuts or the terminal.

### Download and source

Record runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 15 or later. The interface is available in English and Spanish.
