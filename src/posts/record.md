[_Record_](https://github.com/aindaco1/record) is a simple, private screen and audio recorder for Mac, with screenshots built in. It lives in the menu bar, ready to capture creative work without a complicated production setup.
<br><br>

### Supported platforms

Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later.
<br><br>

### What creatives can do

* **Capture a creative process.** Record a screen, window, application, or selected area, along with microphone and computer audio, for tutorials, presentations, performances, demos, reviews, or works in progress.
* **Grab a still and keep working.** Capture a full display, window, application, or selected area. Screenshots save at native resolution as PNGs and copy to the clipboard, ready to paste. JPEG export is available in Settings.
* **Record sound without the screen.** Switch to audio-only mode for conversations, rehearsals, voice notes, and podcast-style recordings.
* **Keep sound flexible for editing.** Microphone and computer audio are saved as separate, uncompressed 24-bit WAV files, making it easier to rebalance voices, music, playback, and demonstrations later.
* **Create a local transcript.** Turn a finished recording into searchable text without sending it to a transcription service. Download and set up Parakeet v3 from Settings once; transcription then runs on your Mac. MacWhisper is also supported when installed with its command-line tool.
* **Clean up the reading copy.** On compatible Macs running macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence enabled, optional transcript cleanup removes fillers and immediate repetitions. The original transcript is kept alongside the reading copy.
* **Recover interrupted sessions.** If a recording stops unexpectedly, Record preserves recoverable material instead of treating the whole session as lost.
* **Keep the capture out of the capture.** Optional controls can hide notifications, Desktop items, or the menu bar while you work.
* **Hand off quickly.** Choose one folder for screenshots and finished sessions, reopen the latest recording, or send a completed video to Gifski when it is installed.
<br><br>

### What's new in 1.4.0

Released September 3, 2026, Record 1.4.0 replaces the record-shaped icon with a 1980s VHS camera. A red dot in the menu bar blinks while you record and disappears when you pause. With Reduce Motion enabled, the recording dot stays steady.

New installations use **Option-Command-Shift-4** to capture an area, avoiding Apple's screenshot shortcut. Existing users keep their shortcuts, including any they've turned off. You can change the Area shortcut or choose **Restore Defaults** in Settings → Screenshots to adopt the new default.
