[_Record_](https://github.com/aindaco1/record) is a simple, private screen and audio recorder for Mac. It is made for capturing creative work without adding a complicated production setup.
<br><br>

### Supported platforms

Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later.
<br><br>

### What creatives can do

* **Capture a creative process.** Record the main screen, microphone, and computer audio for tutorials, presentations, performances, demos, reviews, or works in progress.
* **Record sound without the screen.** Switch to audio-only mode for conversations, rehearsals, voice notes, and podcast-style recordings.
* **Keep sound flexible for editing.** Microphone and computer audio are saved separately, making it easier to rebalance voices, music, playback, and demonstrations later.
* **Create a local transcript.** Turn a finished recording into searchable text without sending the recording to a transcription service.
* **Recover interrupted sessions.** If a recording stops unexpectedly, Record preserves recoverable material instead of treating the whole session as lost.
* **Keep the capture out of the capture.** Optional controls can hide notifications, Desktop items, or Record's menu-bar presence while you work.
* **Hand off quickly.** Choose where finished sessions go, reopen the latest recording, or send a completed video to Gifski when it is installed.
