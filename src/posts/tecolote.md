_Tecolote_ is a short film written and directed by Dust Wave collective member [Nata Aguilar](https://www.instagram.com/natagram505/). Made with a New Mexico cast and crew, it draws on the region's landscape, history, and folklore to bring a witch story into the world of the western.

The film premiered at UNM's Rodey Theatre on May 17, 2026. Its [campaign on The Pool](https://pool.dustwave.xyz/campaigns/tecolote/) reached its $2,000 goal, helping cover production costs, post-production, and festival submissions.
